Interests & Hobbies

  • Cooking: I enjoy cooking various dishes, particularly Ethiopian, Italian, and French cuisines. Cooking is an enjoyable activity mostly because of how creative and calming it is. I usually cook dinner for my family every day and enjoy planning out elaborate meals when I get the chance.
  • Current Events: I spend a lot of my free time reading the news in order to stay informed of current events. This is important to my chosen field of future study, however, I also enjoy staying informed in order to share my knowledge with others. I'm usually able to answer any questions people have about what's going on in the world and have a lot of knowledge to help with my day-to-day academic work.
  • Reading: I enjoy reading novels and short stories. Due to my busy schedule, I usually don't have a lot of time to read, however, whenever I am on break from school I take some time to read. My favorite genres are dystopian and fantasy as they usually present scenarios that are incredibly interesting.
  • Watching T.V. and Movies: I enjoy watching longer T.V. serieses that have complex and developed plots. Unlike reading, I do get the chance to watch T.V. often because I can spend time with others and watch T.V. at the same time. My favorite shows currently are Suits and Designated Survivor.
  • Travel: While I don't get to travel often, I always love going to new places. Even if I don't go a long distance, it's an incredibly fun activity, however, I do prefer international travel. I enjoy learning about new places, cultures, and the history of wherever I visit. Also, I enjoy trying new foods and learning about new cooking styles.
